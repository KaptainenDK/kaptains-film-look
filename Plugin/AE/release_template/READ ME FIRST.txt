KAPTAINS FILM LOOK  -  film look for After Effects and Premiere Pro (Mac, Apple silicon)
Lens distortion, chromatic aberration, lens blur, vignette, bloom, halation and grain, with zones.
Free and open source (GPL v3). Development version (0.1.0).

INSTALL
1. Unzip the download (double-click the .zip). Keep all the files together in one folder.
2. Open "Install Kaptains Film Look.command".
   macOS may say it cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer. Then: right-click the file > Open > Open.
   (Or in Terminal:  xattr -cr "<the unzipped folder>"  and open it again.)
3. Type your Mac password when asked (the plug-in goes in the shared Adobe folder, so it works for both programs).
4. Quit and restart After Effects / Premiere Pro.

WHERE TO FIND IT
After Effects: Effect > Kaptain > Kaptains Film Look.
Premiere Pro:  Effects panel > Video Effects > Kaptain > Kaptains Film Look.

GOOD TO KNOW
- After Effects uses the GPU (Metal). Premiere Pro currently renders the effect on the CPU, so heavy settings can be slower there.
- Needs an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer). Intel Macs and Windows are not supported yet.
- The plug-in is not notarized by Apple (it is a free development build), which is why steps 2-3 above are needed.
- "Show Zones" in Premiere is burned into the picture: turn it off before rendering.

Move  /Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/Plug-ins/7.0/MediaCore/KaptainsFilmLook.plugin  to the Trash and restart the Adobe apps.
